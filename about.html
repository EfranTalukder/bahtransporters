Who we are

A bridge between Birmingham and Tallinding.

Bah Transporters Ltd is a UK-registered company (no. 14556620) running air freight in both directions between the UK and The Gambia. You drop off at 77 Melbourne Avenue in Newtown, Birmingham; your family collects at Nusrat Junction, Tallinding. Or the other way round.

A parcel to The Gambia is rarely “just a parcel”. It's school shoes before term, medicine for a parent, a phone someone has saved for. So we keep things simple, honest and personal: one fair price per kilo, a real flight date on every booking, and a team that answers.

What we stand for

Four promises on every shipment.

01

Fair

One clear rate — £11.99 per kg, 1 kg minimum. You see the price before you pack and pay only after we weigh.

02

Personal

You talk to people, not a chatbot. The same small team answers your WhatsApp in the UK and in The Gambia.

03

Careful

Every parcel is checked and, where needed, repacked to international airline rules before it flies.

04

Dependable

Every booking is tied to a real flight date, and you can follow it with your reference at any time.

The journey

From your hands to theirs.

  1. 1

    Checked & packed

    We weigh, check and — where needed — repack your parcel to airline rules, then confirm your final price.

  2. 2

    Flown on your date

    It travels on the flight you booked. If an airline changes a schedule, we message you straight away.

  3. 3

    Collected with a smile

    Your receiver collects in Tallinding by quoting your reference — or we deliver on request.
