How much does it cost to send a parcel?

£11.99 per kilo in both directions, with a 1 kg minimum per parcel — so a 400 g parcel costs the same as 1 kg. Documents are £15 flat. Some items, such as phones, can carry a small flat handling charge; we always confirm the final amount before you pay.

When and how do I pay?

After we weigh your parcel. We send you the final amount and you pay by bank transfer or cash. Payment must be complete before your shipment is delivered or collected.

How do I know which flight my parcel is on?

Every booking is tied to a flight. When you book you get a reference like BAH-7K2QAF — enter it on our Track page any time to see where your shipment is.

Can you collect from me or deliver to my family?

Yes. We can arrange collection in the UK and delivery in The Gambia on request. Choose it when you book and we'll confirm the cost with you before anything moves.

Can I send from The Gambia to the UK?

Absolutely. Drop off at Nusrat Junction in Tallinding; your receiver collects in Birmingham, or we post it on to their UK address.

Is my parcel insured?

Shipments aren't insured by default. If an airline loses or damages items, we refund once we receive the airline's compensation, as set out in our terms. Please pack valuables carefully and declare their value when you book.

Who pays customs duties?

You choose: pay any duties upfront, or let the receiver pay on arrival. Tell us when you book.

What if a flight date changes?

We work hard to keep every date, but airlines sometimes change schedules. If that happens we message you straight away on WhatsApp with the new plan.
