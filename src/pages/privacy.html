Privacy policy

Your details, handled with care.

What we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have. Last updated 15 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Bah Transporters Ltd (company no. 14556620) is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about anything in this policy at info@bahtransporters.com or on WhatsApp +44 7982 144203.

2. Information we collect

  • Identification and contact details: names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses of senders and receivers.
  • Booking details: what you are sending, its weight and value, photos you choose to upload, your booking reference and flight.
  • Payment records: amounts, dates and payment method. We do not take or store card details on this website.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by phone or email.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly, in a scrambled form, to protect our forms from abuse.

3. How we use it

  • To provide our service: processing bookings, shipping, collection, delivery and tracking (contract).
  • To contact you about your shipment and answer your questions (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To keep accounting and tax records and meet legal obligations (legal obligation).
  • To keep our website and service secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interests).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only where needed to run our service: airlines and customs authorities; our technology providers for secure database and website hosting; and legal authorities where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as needed to provide our service and meet UK accounting and tax rules (normally six years). Photos uploaded with a booking are deleted automatically after a year.

6. Security

Your data is stored in a secure database with access restricted to our staff, who each sign in with their own login. Tracking only shows prices and your customer number to someone who also knows the phone number you booked with.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may remember your last booking reference on your own device so you can track it quickly.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Contact us to use these rights. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes appear on this page with a new date.
