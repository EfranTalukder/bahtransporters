Before you book

Air freight booking rules

These are the rules you accept when you tick the box on the booking page. They are printed on every invoice too.

  1. Pack securely, especially anything fragile — we may repack to airline rules.
  2. Describe the contents and their value honestly for customs.
  3. Double-check the receiver’s name and phone number.
  4. Parcels are £11.99 per kg with a 1 kg minimum; documents are £15 flat. The final price is confirmed when we weigh it.
  5. You pay after weigh-in, by bank transfer or cash, before delivery or collection.
  6. No perishable food, seeds or plants, flammable liquids or perfumes, narcotics, or firearms and accessories.
  7. Shipments are not insured. If an airline loses or damages items, we refund once the airline compensates us.
  8. Airlines sometimes change schedules — if your flight date moves, we message you on WhatsApp straight away.

1. Who we are

These terms apply to every shipment booked with Bah Transporters Ltd (company no. 14556620, England & Wales), “we” or “us”. By booking online, by phone, on WhatsApp or in person you agree to them. Contact us at info@bahtransporters.com or +44 7982 144203.

2. Bookings and references

When you book you receive a booking reference (for example BAH-7K2QAF). It identifies your shipment. Please quote it whenever you contact us, drop off, collect or pay.

A booking reserves space on a flight. It becomes final once we have received, weighed and checked your shipment. You must give accurate sender and receiver names and phone numbers.

3. Prices and payment

  • Parcels are charged at £11.99 per kilogram, with a minimum charge of 1 kg per parcel. Anything under 1 kg is charged as 1 kg.
  • Documents are charged at a flat £15. Some items (for example phones) may carry an agreed flat handling charge.
  • For items we buy on your behalf, you pay the item cost plus shipping.
  • Prices shown when you book are estimates. Your final price is confirmed after we weigh your shipment.
  • Shipment costs must be paid before delivery or collection can be completed.
  • Collection and delivery services are priced separately and confirmed with you in advance.

4. Items we can't carry

You must not send perishable items or food; seeds or plants; flammable liquids or perfumes; narcotics; firearms and accessories; or anything else restricted by the airline, UK or Gambian customs rules.

We may refuse, open for inspection or return any shipment we reasonably believe breaks these rules. You are responsible for any costs, fines or delays caused by prohibited or undeclared items.

5. Packing, contents and customs

  • Pack your items securely, especially anything fragile. We may repack shipments to meet international airline rules.
  • Describe the contents and their value accurately; customs may require it.
  • You may choose to pay customs duties or taxes upfront, or the receiver may pay on arrival.
  • Check that the receiver's name, phone number and any delivery address are correct to avoid delays.

6. Insurance and liability

Your items are not insured. Occasionally airlines may misplace or damage bags or items. We cannot be held liable if your items are lost or damaged.

Where an airline pays a refund or compensation for lost or damaged items, we will refund their cost once we have received that refund or compensation from the airline, which can take up to 24 months.

7. Flight dates

We make every effort to keep to agreed departure and arrival dates, but outside factors can affect scheduling. We do not accept responsibility for any failure to keep to agreed departure or arrival dates. If your flight changes, we will tell you as soon as we can.

8. Drop-off, collection and delivery

UK drop-off: 77 Melbourne Avenue, Newtown, Birmingham B19 2HU, United Kingdom. Gambia pick-up: Nusrat Junction, Tallinding, The Gambia.

Receivers collect by quoting the booking reference. Where you ask us to post or deliver a shipment, we will confirm the cost before it is sent.

9. Refunds

Refunds can only be issued to the account that made the original payment. We cannot process refunds to third parties.

10. Your information

We use the details you give us to provide our service. See our Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to these terms

These terms may change. The version on this page applies to bookings made from the date shown at the top.
